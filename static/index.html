The Problem

Payment pricing isn't one percentage. Contracts contain tiers, caps, waivers, GST, and transaction-specific exceptions.

Reconciliation means checking those rules against every settlement. Manual review doesn't scale, so discrepancies go unnoticed.

Pipeline
01

Contract

Upload the merchant agreement as a PDF. Every clause that touches pricing — tiers, caps, waivers, exceptions — gets pulled out as a candidate rule.

LLM: contract interpretation
02

Rules

Clauses become versioned, structured rules with a source citation attached to each one — auditable and diffable, not fee logic buried in prose.

LLM: rule extraction
03

Reconcile

Every settled transaction in the CSV is recomputed against the rule set. Fee math runs in code, not the model, so results are deterministic and repeatable.

Code: fee calculation
04

Evidence

Every mismatch ships with the expected fee, the charged fee, the delta, and the exact contract clause it violates — no black-box output.

Output: audit trail
